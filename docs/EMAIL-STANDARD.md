# Email standard for every project (Atilla, 26 Oct 2026)

"Every agent's response and approach must be a masterclass in how to write an email."

This applies to EVERY email: cold, follow-up, every reply to a prospect or customer, and every service note.

## Before writing
1. Know exactly what this reader wants most right now, and what they fear. Write to that, not to our product.
2. Know the ONE thing this email must make them do next.

## Structure
- **Subject:** the reader's own words, a question they answer in their head, or their concrete situation. Never a label.
- **Line 1:** about them, never about us. No "I hope you're well", "My name is", "I wanted to reach out" or "Just following up".
- **Body:** mirror their moment, then the loss or the stakes, then exactly how we remove it, then one true proof.
- **Ask:** one clear next step that is easy to say yes to (a one-word reply, a specific time, a specific document).
- **Length:** under 110 words for cold. A reply is as short as the answer allows. Answer their question in the FIRST sentence.

## Voice
Certain, warm, short sentences. Founder-to-owner. No hedging ("might", "perhaps", "I'd guess", "just"). No apologies except for a real error, once. No jargon they wouldn't use.

## Replies to a positive or curious lead
- Answer exactly what they asked, first.
- Give before asking: a finding, a tender, a recording, an audit result made for THEM.
- Make the next step concrete and dated ("I'll send it by 10:00 tomorrow" / "Reply 'yes' and I start today").
- Bring in the price only when they ask, or when the value has been shown, stated plainly with what's included.

## Truth and law (non-negotiable)
- Every claim is true and provable. No invented numbers, customers, results, fake scarcity, fake "Re:", or endorsement implications (Google, Etsy, Shopify, MaPS, UJP).
- Footer on cold email: sender identity, postal address, and the opt-out ("reply 'no'").
- Follow each project's existing rules: İYS/6563, PECR (limited companies only), CAN-SPAM, and no-call where it applies.

## Quality gate
- **Cold and follow-up templates:** reviewed by Orchestrator plus an independent copy-chief agent before scale.
- **Any reply to a warm lead (positive, a question, a price ask):** before sending, run it past a fresh reviewer sub-agent (no context, the copy only) with this file as the rubric. Fix it, then send.
- **Weekly:** each project reports its reply rate per template. Below 3% means rewrite, not resend.
